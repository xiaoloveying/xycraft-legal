XYCraft Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

XYCraft is a drawing app. This policy explains how the current Android version handles data.

Artwork and settings. The artwork, layers, palettes, brush settings, and app preferences you create are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. This version does not require an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or upload this data to XYCraft servers; it does not request internet permission. Artwork may contain personal information you choose to add, so please handle it carefully.

Import and export. The app reads images, projects, or palettes that you choose through the system file picker. When you export, the data is written to the destination you select. That destination may be a third-party app or cloud storage; subsequent handling is governed by the relevant provider. Check the content and destination before sharing.

Device backups. The app allows Android backup. If backup is enabled for your device and account, the system may back up app data to your backup service, such as your Google account. Whether backups occur, how long they are kept, and how to delete them depend on your device settings and the service provider’s policies.

Retention and deletion. Artwork and settings on your device remain until you delete the artwork, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Export any artwork you want to keep before uninstalling. Backups and exported copies must be deleted separately from their respective storage locations.

Contact. For privacy questions, email xycraft@agent.qq.com.

This policy may be updated as features or data handling change. Please consult the latest policy in the app and linked from Google Play.
